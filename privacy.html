Privacy notice

Last updated 5 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information Voldato collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, your email address and your message.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or third-party fonts.

How we use it

We use your details only to read and reply to your enquiry. We do not sell your information, add you to a mailing list or use it for marketing without asking you first.

Who handles it

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to our inbox. It processes your message on our behalf and may store it on servers outside your country.
  • GitHub hosts this website. Like most hosts, it may record technical information such as your IP address in server logs to keep the service running and secure.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry for as long as we need it to deal with your request and any follow-up conversation, then delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, contact us through the contact form on our homepage.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
